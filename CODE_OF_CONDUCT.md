# AccessForge Code of Conduct

AccessForge aims to be a respectful, accessible, and safety-minded community. We welcome contributions from disabled people, caregivers, makers, engineers, researchers, and people learning these areas.

Expected behavior:

- listen to lived experience without demanding disclosure or proof
- use people’s stated names, pronouns, and preferred disability language
- explain disagreement with evidence and curiosity
- make space for different communication styles and access needs
- report hazards, privacy concerns, and accessibility barriers early
- credit contributors and compensate lived-experience work when the project funds it

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, ableism, discrimination, sexualized or violent content, doxxing, sharing private data, pressuring someone to disclose health information, dismissing safety reports, or submitting unsafe designs as jokes or experiments.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Retaliation is not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate, remove, or suspend participation while preserving an appeal path.
